Human genetics ran that trial for free. Finding the next one means combining evidence that lives in separate places: rare-variant burden studies, GWAS, population tolerance data and effects on other traits. No single source answers the question.

Does a collective of agents, each seeing one slice and coordinating with no planner, beat one agent that sees everything?

The system

Five agents. Each can open one folder of evidence.

Round 0Four specialists post verdicts independently and broadcast needs for what they cannot see.
Round 1Each agent bids on the open need it can answer, ranked by 2·novelty + centrality + 0.5·depth + 0.2·age.
Round 2The skeptic challenges weak verdicts; challenged or answered agents may revise.
ScorePlain Python sums the latest verdicts. A challenged channel counts half.

Limitations, in full

    Where this sits

    Placed against the literature, not ranked against it.

    On coordination. A systematic evaluation of five multi-agent debate methods across nine benchmarks and four foundation models found they did not reliably beat simple single-agent baselines such as chain-of-thought and self-consistency (Stop Overvaluing Multi-Agent Debate). Debate can also degrade performance, notably when weaker agents join stronger ones (Talk Isn't Always Cheap). Both study agents answering the same question. Here the agents hold disjoint evidence, and the loop still costs accuracy, with the mechanism measured.
    On simple baselines. Across 445 diseases, neither eQTL colocalization nor Open Targets' locus-to-gene score outperformed the plain nearest-gene method at prioritizing future drug targets (medRxiv 2025). Our scripted baseline S4 is competitive with our agents, which is what that literature would predict.
    On this event's own framework. We use the ScienceClaw artifact-and-needs layer and its pressure formula verbatim, 2·novelty + centrality + 0.5·depth + 0.2·age (arXiv:2603.14312), reimplemented standalone; our board skill drops into that ecosystem.
    We do not compare our AUROC to published target-prediction numbers. Different panel, different task.
