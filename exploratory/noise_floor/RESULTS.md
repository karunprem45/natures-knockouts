## The coordination penalty in every replicate

Each replicate's own board, re-scored round 0 only with the unchanged `scoring.py`, gives an independent measurement of the coordination penalty (A3 − round 0).

| Seed | A3 AUROC | Round 0 AUROC | A3 − round 0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0.790 | 0.842 | -0.052 |
| 2 | 0.774 | 0.865 | -0.091 |
| 3 | 0.829 | 0.869 | -0.040 |
| 4 | 0.842 | 0.856 | -0.013 |
| 5 | 0.787 | 0.857 | -0.070 |

Penalty across replicates: mean -0.053, sd 0.029, negative in 5 of 5. If the loop had no effect, each replicate would be equally likely to go either way; all 5 negative has one-sided sign-test probability 1/32 = 0.031.

**What changed versus what we said before.** The earlier ~0.03 floor came from one accidental pair of runs; measured over 10 pairs it is 0.062, about twice as large. Against it the frozen coordination penalty (0.071) is only 1.1× the floor. The penalty's sign, however, replicates in every run, and its size across replicates (mean 0.053) is smaller than the frozen seeds suggested.

With 10 pairwise differences, the 95th percentile sits close to the maximum, so this floor is deliberately conservative.
