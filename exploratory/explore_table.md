## Secondary, not the pre-registered headline

The pre-registered headline is **A3 − A1 = +0.008 (−0.074, +0.103)** on Gemini, from [`frozen_results/arms_table.md`](../frozen_results/arms_table.md). A3 round 0 (isolated specialists) is one of the pre-registered ablations (`frozen/metrics.md`: "A3_isolated (round 0 only)"), but choosing it as the comparison to highlight was done after seeing results. Paired bootstrap, 1,000 gene resamples, seed 20261031; "CI excludes 0" means the 95% interval does not cross zero.

Cross-check: the Gemini A3 round 0 − A1 delta recomputed here is +0.080 (-0.004, +0.173), matching the frozen arms table (+0.080, −0.004 to +0.173).

**Reading it honestly:** isolated specialists beat the single agent by about +0.08 on Gemini and +0.04 on gpt-5-mini, but neither interval excludes zero (Gemini's lower bound is −0.004). The one interval that excludes zero is the **coordination penalty on Gemini: A3 − A3 round 0 = −0.071 (−0.139, −0.013)**; on gpt-5-mini the same contrast is −0.106 with an interval just crossing zero. Direction is consistent across two models. These are several post-hoc contrasts on 40 genes with no multiplicity correction, so they are evidence for the next pre-registered test, not settled effects.
